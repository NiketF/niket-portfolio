About

A builder who likes the space between an interesting AI idea and the details required to make it useful.

I work on AI applications across LLM workflows, NLP, automation, document intelligence, and user-facing product experiences. I am especially interested in building systems that can reason with information without losing sight of how that information should be handled.

Alongside AI engineering, I have formal foundations in cybersecurity through my degree specialization. That gives me a practical second lens on the systems I build, from privacy and data handling to access, trust, and responsible use of AI. I bring those principles into product decisions without losing focus on usability or delivery.

I enjoy the full loop: understanding a problem, prototyping the workflow, testing the experience, then tightening the system until it feels obvious to use.
